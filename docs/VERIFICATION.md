# Verification standards

## Status meanings

| Status | Meaning | Display rule |
|---|---|---|
| `official` | Reddit Help Center or an official Reddit announcement directly supports the entry. | May be described as officially documented. |
| `community` | A community-maintained list, user report, or discussion supports the entry. | Clearly label as community-reported. |
| `historical` | A reliable source documents a past state or discontinued feature. | Show the relevant date; never imply it is currently obtainable. |
| `unverified` | A lead without sufficient corroboration. | Keep out of confirmed totals. |

## Evidence rules

1. Prefer current Reddit Help Center pages and official Reddit announcements.
2. Preserve the source's exact threshold and scope. Do not fill gaps with guesses.
3. Record when a source was reviewed.
4. Separate achievements, Trophy Case trophies, and post/comment awards.
5. If community reports conflict, retain the disagreement in notes and link both sources.
6. Never claim a global total based on a partial list or a single user report.
7. A feature being visible to one account does not prove universal availability.
8. Do not claim an item is retired unless there is evidence of retirement.

## Known official behaviour

Reddit says achievements are visible from the profile's Achievements area. Users can hide individual achievement badges. Rarity indicates the difficulty level, and an unlock number indicates the user's ordinal unlock position. Streak progress begins when the feature becomes available on that account.

Source: [What are achievements?](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/27063106698004-What-are-achievements)
